Presence Detection Zones
Monitor RSSI fluctuations between mesh devices to detect room occupancy
How it works: When you move through a room, your body affects the radio signals between Zigbee devices. Zones detect these RSSI fluctuations to determine occupancy without dedicated sensors. Best with mains-powered devices (lights, plugs, switches).
